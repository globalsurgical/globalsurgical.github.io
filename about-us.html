About Global Surgical

Global Surgical (গ্লোবাল সার্জিক্যাল অ্যান্ড ইন্সট্রুমেন্টস) is a trusted name in Habiganj, Bangladesh, operating as a reliable wholesale and retail distributor of medical devices, surgical instruments, and laboratory supplies.

Our Mission

To provide hospitals, clinics, diagnostic laboratories, and healthcare professionals with uncompromised, top-quality medical equipment to ensure precise diagnostics and safe patient treatments.

Quality Control & Sourcing Integrity

We source our inventory directly from reputed and certified international and local manufacturers, strictly adhering to safety benchmarks and regulatory guidelines.
