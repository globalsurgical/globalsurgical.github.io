Who We Are

Global Surgical is a premier wholesale supplier and distributor committed to delivering top-tier medical appliances, surgical instruments, diagnostic films, and pathology reagents. Headquartered in Habiganj, Bangladesh, we bridge the gap between world-class medical equipment manufacturers and healthcare institutions to ensure safety and precision in patient care.

We take pride in being a comprehensive solutions provider, catering to the dynamic needs of modern hospitals, diagnostic centers, clinical laboratories, and pharmacies. By offering a vast portfolio of both local and foreign medical instruments, we ensure that healthcare professionals have access to the exact tools they need.
